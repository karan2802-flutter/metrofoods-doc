Delete Your Metro Foods Account

Metro Foods allows you to permanently delete your account and associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Metro Foods app.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your request.

If you cannot access your account, email sales@metrofoods.co.nz.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account
  • Profile information
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Personal information
  • Order history

Data Retention

Unless required by law, your personal data will be permanently deleted after processing your request.
