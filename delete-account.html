Delete Your Metro Foods Account

Metro Foods is committed to protecting your privacy. You have full control over your personal data and can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account via the App

  1. Log in to your Metro Foods app.
  2. Tap the Profile icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Select the My Profile tab at the top.
  4. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  5. Tap Delete Account and confirm your action.

Request Deletion via Email (Web Alternative)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team:

Email us at: sales@metrofoods.co.nz with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email address.

Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted

  • Personal details (Name, Email address, Phone number)
  • Saved delivery addresses and business details
  • Active login sessions and credentials
  • App preferences and custom settings

Data Retention & Compliance

Account deletion is irreversible. Your account access will be revoked immediately upon request.

Certain transactional and tax records (such as completed invoice history) may be retained for the minimum period required by New Zealand tax and business compliance laws.
