Privacy & Policies

Effective Date: 1st April, 2026

Welcome to Metro Foods, the premier destination for wholesale distribution of high-quality food products, cleaning essentials, and premium packaging solutions. This Privacy Policy outlines how Metrofoods, operating under the brand name MRS World Ltd., collects, uses, and protects the information provided by users of our website.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you use the Metro Foods website, we may collect personal information such as your name, contact information, delivery address, and other details necessary for processing your wholesale orders.

Order Information

We collect information related to your orders, including the products ordered, quantity, and transaction details. This information is used for order processing, delivery, and record-keeping.

Usage Data

We may collect information about how you interact with our website and app, including your browsing history, preferences, and other usage data. This helps us enhance user experience and improve our services.

How We Use Your Information

Order Processing

We use the information collected to process and fulfil your wholesale orders, communicate with you, and provide customer support.

Improving Our Services

We analyse usage data to improve our website and app functionality, tailor our product offerings, and enhance user experience.

Information Sharing

Metro Foods does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted partners and service providers involved in order fulfilment, delivery, and customer support.

Data Security

We take appropriate measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. Our website and app use industry-standard encryption protocols to protect sensitive data.

User Access and Control

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can request deletion of your account directly from the app by navigating to Profile → Delete Account, or by contacting us at sales@metrofoods.co.nz.

Notifications

Metro Foods uses notifications only to inform you when an invoice download has been completed. Notifications are shown only after you grant permission on your device. If you tap the notification, the downloaded invoice will be opened. You can enable or disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

File Downloads

Metro Foods allows users to download invoices generated through the app. Downloaded invoices are stored on your device for your convenience. The app only opens an invoice when you choose to open it, such as by tapping the download notification or selecting the downloaded file within the app. Metro Foods does not access, collect, or share your downloaded invoices.

Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services provided by Google, to understand app usage, monitor app performance, and identify and fix crashes and technical issues. These services may automatically collect technical and usage information such as device information, app version, operating system information, app interactions, and crash diagnostics. This information is used to improve the functionality, performance, and stability of our application.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

Metro Foods reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy as needed. Changes will be effective upon posting on our website. We encourage users to review this policy periodically for any updates.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us at sales@metrofoods.co.nz.

Thank you for choosing Metro Foods!
