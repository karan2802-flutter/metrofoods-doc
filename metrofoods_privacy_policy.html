User Access and Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data. You can delete your account and associated records directly inside the app, or request deletion via email[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span):

How to Delete Your Account via the App:

  1. Log in to your Metro Foods app.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Select the My Profile section.
  4. Scroll down to the Danger Zone area.
  5. Tap Delete Account and confirm your request.

Request Deletion via Email (Web Alternative):

If you cannot access the app, you can submit an account deletion request by emailing us directly at sales@metrofoods.co.nz from your registered email address[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).

Data That Will Be Deleted:

  • Profile data (Name, Registered Email, Phone number)
  • Saved delivery and billing addresses
  • Active authentication sessions and tokens

Data Retention Terms:

Account deletion takes effect immediately. Completed invoice and commercial transactional history may be retained for statutory periods required under New Zealand financial and taxation laws.
